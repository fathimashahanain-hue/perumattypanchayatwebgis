About Perumatty

A beautiful agricultural landscape in Palakkad district, Kerala

Introduction

Perumatty Grama Panchayat is situated in Chittur Block of Palakkad district in Kerala, near the interstate boundary with Tamil Nadu. It is an important rural administrative region known for its agricultural landscape and village settlements.

Agriculture plays an important role in the local economy. Paddy cultivation, irrigation facilities, rural roads, residential areas and natural resources form important elements of the Panchayat's landscape.

This website presents the geographical characteristics of Perumatty and provides an interactive WebGIS platform to explore spatial information and understand the Panchayat through digital mapping.

Geographical Information

Understanding the spatial and physical characteristics

📍 Geographical Location

Perumatty is located in Palakkad district, Kerala, within Chittur Block Panchayat. It lies close to the Tamil Nadu border.

🌾 Physical Landscape

The region includes agricultural plains, paddy fields, settlements and cultivated lands. The landscape supports rural livelihoods and agricultural activities.

💧 Water Resources

Irrigation canals, reservoirs, ponds and groundwater resources are important components of the local agricultural environment.

🌱 Land Use and Agriculture

Agriculture is an important land-use activity. Paddy fields and other cultivated lands contribute to the rural economy of the region.

🏘️ Settlement Pattern

The Panchayat consists of rural settlements, residential areas, agricultural properties, local roads and community facilities.

🏛️ Administrative Details

District: Palakkad
Block: Chittur
Panchayat: Perumatty
Number of wards: 18
